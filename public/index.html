💰 1. Currencies & Economy

• Coins (🪙): Earned by selling rolled numbers (1 Rarity = 1 Coin; negative numbers sold also award positive Coins) and claiming >daily rewards. Used for Potions, Modules, and Chips. Large numbers format cleanly into xyz.ab Suffix (K, M, B, T, Qa, Qi, Sx, Sp, Oc, No, Dc, etc.).

• Crystals (💎): Obtained via >daily drops, Achievements, Crystal Maker (2% chance / guaranteed at 75th roll), Crystal Maker-Kit (100% guaranteed), and Crystal Module (3% chance / 34 pity, or 3.5% / 29 pity when paired with Crystal Chip). Also used to expand Inventory slots (cost = 2^(current_slots - 10)).

• NULL (⬛): Anomaly currency obtained at a 1.404% chance every 19 rolls when the result is a negative number. Used for negative-luck Potions and Malfunction Module crafting.

• Compressed Crystal (💠 CC): Currency purchased in the Black Market for 1,000 Crystals (stock of 5, 10, 20, or 50) or in the Quest Token Shop for 15 Quest Tokens (limit 1/week, requires >25,000 rolls).

🎲 2. Luck Formula, Roll Mechanics & Potion Ranks

Luck = (((1 + Basic Luck) * Multi Luck) + Special Luck) * FINAL MULTIPLIER

• Base Cooldown: 10.00s (minimum 1.00s).

• Potion Rank Rules: Potions with a numeric Rank (e.g., Rank 1.0, Rank -2.0) allow only 1 active potion per rank at a time and cannot overwrite a different potion of the same rank. Consuming the exact same potion extends its remaining roll duration. Special Rank potions can be used at any time and stack freely with all ranks!

⚙️ 3. Modules, Chips & Inventory Storage

• You can equip 1 Primary Module and 1 Secondary Chip at a time. Important: Unequipping a Module or Chip resets its accumulated roll counter/stacks back to 0!

• Auto-Roll: Equip Auto-Roll Machine (pauses every 1,337 rolls until you roll manually once).

• Inventory & Auto-Sell: You start with 10 slots. If your inventory is full when a new number is rolled, the smallest unlocked number is automatically sold. Lock your best numbers so they are never replaced, and configure positive/negative Auto-Sell thresholds in Settings.

👑 4. Rolling Titles

• Equip Spectrometer Module to roll Titles alongside numbers, from [Sol] (1 in 1) up to [S p e c t r u m] (1 in 10,000)!

🌍 5. Daily Progression, Shrine Pray & Black Market

• Daily Rewards (>daily): Resets at 00:00 UTC. Scales across 5 tiers based on your Total Rolls (Tier 1: <1k rolls up to Tier 5: 1,000,000+ rolls with overflow Crystal chances).

• Shrine Pray (>pray): Pray once every 6 hours to permanently gain +0.01 Basic Luck.

• Daily Quests (>quest): Complete 3 daily quests to earn Coins, Potions, and Quest Tokens (🎫) (plus 1 free Reroll per day).

• Black Market (>bshop): Spawns randomly every 60s (0.1% chance) and stays open for 10 minutes. Offers a 25% discount on standard shop items (100 limit on unlimited items) and exclusive items (Compressed Crystal, Crystal Maker-Kit, Reality Potion, Oblivion Potion). Spawns are delivered via private Discord DMs to opted-in players!
